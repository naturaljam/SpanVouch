\begin{abstract}
Diagnosing failures in tool-using agents requires more than locating a suspicious
step: a trustworthy report must identify a causal failure, bind each claim to
re-addressable trajectory evidence, and expose when that evidence is
insufficient. This Technical Report presents \method, Independently Verified
Agent Diagnosis, as an evidence-constrained selective decision. IVAD combines a
versioned claim--evidence contract, deterministic integrity and scope checks, a
separated semantic verifier, one bounded revision, and explicit abstention or
human review. It also specifies a group-selective risk protocol with
simultaneous exact-binomial bounds over a frozen finite candidate family.
\system realizes the protocol with six public contract roots, recoverable review
state, framework and provider adapters, and manifest-bound artifacts. The latest
formal evidence is DeepSeek-only: 2,148 scheduled/evaluated plans, zero missing
cells, completed B0--B3 conditions, and policy-skipped B4/B5 conditions with no
Qwen results. The observed B3-versus-B2 risk and coverage differences are a
risk--coverage tradeoff under this fixed run, not causal, cross-model, or
certificate evidence. H1--H5 remain unresolved.
\end{abstract}
